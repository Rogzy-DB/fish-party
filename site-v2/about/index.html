Where it started

It began at a party

In June 2026 we put a colouring table in a corner of a family party and a projector on the wall. Guests of every age coloured paper fish, a scanner sent them to the tank, and by the end of the night 46 creatures were swimming together.

Their drawings still swim in our party tank. We removed the guests' names before putting it online.

The inspiration

teamLab's Sketch Aquarium

The idea is not ours. Sketch Aquarium is an artwork by the art collective teamLab: children colour sea creatures, and they come to life in a giant digital aquarium. If you ever get the chance, go and see the real one. Fish Party is a small, free homage you can run on one laptop; it is not affiliated with teamLab.

Video: Sketch Aquarium © teamLab, from their official channel.
