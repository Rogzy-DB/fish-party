How it works

From a pencil to the deep sea

🖍️

Colour

One of 12 sea creatures, on a printed sheet or straight on a phone.

📷

Send

Snap the sheet or tap “Swim!”. The tank finds your creature, cuts it out and turns it the right way up.

🐟

Swim

Each species moves its own way: jellyfish pulse, sharks cruise and strike, the octopus ripples its arms.
